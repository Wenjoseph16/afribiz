# AfriBiz - Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Mission:** Empower African businesses with an all-in-one SaaS platform for growth, commerce, and management.

**Vision:** The operating system for African entrepreneurship.

**Tagline:** Propulsez votre business en Afrique

**Brand Personality:** Ambitious, Trustworthy, Innovative, Warm, Pan-African

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- Primary: Emerald mark + "AfriBiz" wordmark in Space Grotesk Bold
- Monochrome: White on dark, black on light
- Favicon: Stylized "A" mark in emerald

### Iconography
- Library: Lucide React (stroke-width 1.5)
- Size: 16px (inline), 20px (UI), 24px (section), 32px (hero)
- All icons must have aria-labels
- No emoji as icons

### Voice & Tone
- **Voice**: Confident, warm, direct, professional
- **Tone**: Encouraging (onboarding), Direct (dashboard), Warm (marketing), Authoritative (admin)
- **Language**: French (primary), English (secondary)
- **Do**: Use active voice, short sentences, plain language
- **Don't**: Use jargon, corporate speak, overly casual language

### Logo Usage
- Clear space: 1x logo height on all sides
- Minimum size: 24px (favicon), 120px (header)
- Don't: Recolor, add effects, rotate, place on low-contrast backgrounds
- Preferred: Emerald logo on white/light bg, White logo on dark bg

## Design Principles
1. **Mobile-first Africa**: Design for smartphone-first users, low bandwidth
2. **Trust through design**: Clean, professional, secure-feeling interfaces
3. **Progressivity**: Simple for beginners, powerful for experts
4. **Pan-African identity**: Celebrate African aesthetics without stereotypes
5. **Performance is a feature**: Fast load times, even on 3G
